YOUR WINDOWS DESKTOP, THROUGHOUT THE HOUSE

One powerful computer. A workspace in every room.

Imagine a tiny, low-power computer mounted behind your monitor. Add a keyboard and mouse, then connect over your home network to a full Windows desktop running on your main server. Your desk stays uncluttered while the processing happens on the server.

Set up a screen in your office, bedroom, workshop or study. Each supported thin client could access an appropriate Windows or Linux virtual desktop—without needing a full-size PC at every desk.

Planned capability: SamyStackOS aims to manage the server-side virtual machines and compatible client connections. Windows licensing, remote-desktop support, performance and concurrent sessions depend on your hardware, software and configuration.
