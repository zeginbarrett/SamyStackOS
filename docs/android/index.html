ANDROID COMPUTE

More power.
Less waste.

Turn compatible Android phones into a distributed processing fleet. Connect them to SamyStackOS over USB or Wi-Fi, and let a central coordinator put their processors to work on supported jobs—right alongside your mini PCs and Raspberry Pis.

In development · Explore the vision
A NEW ROLE FOR EXISTING HARDWARE

Big ideas don’t always need big machines.

Even a spare smartphone has a capable processor, usable memory and a network connection. Instead of leaving those resources idle, SamyStackOS is being designed to bring them together in one coordinated computing system.

Meet the pocket-sized compute fleet.

  • Install the SamyStack Node app on supported Android phones
  • Connect through a powered USB data hub or your local Wi-Fi
  • Authorize each device with the Debian-based cluster controller
  • Let the scheduler match jobs to available CPU, memory and supported acceleration
  • Watch work progress across the fleet from one dashboard

HOW ANDROID JOINING WORKS

Plug in. Pair up. Put it to work.

The Debian coordinator manages the job queue while each Android phone runs its own lightweight worker. No complicated manual assignment is needed for every task.

Connect

Install the Android app and connect the phone by USB data cable or local Wi-Fi.

Authorize

Approve the new worker in SamyStackOS and set its resource and charging limits.

Compute

The scheduler sends supported tasks to eligible phones and tracks progress.

Complete

Results return to the coordinator, ready for the next stage of the workload.

FLEXIBLE CONNECTIONS

Built for the shelf, the desk or a full phone fleet.

Phones can stay in one place and work together, or join over your home network. SamyStackOS is designed to find the best available connection without making you manage every device separately.

⛓USB DATA + POWER

One cable. Two jobs.

A compatible powered USB hub can keep phones charged while providing a wired data connection to the Debian host. A neat, predictable setup for permanently mounted worker phones.

◉WIRELESS WORKERS

Same cluster. No fixed location.

Phones can also communicate over local Wi-Fi, making it easy to add another available device without rebuilding your hardware setup.

DESIGNED FOR REAL DEVICES

Useful power, without burning out your phones.

Each worker is designed around practical limits: temperature, battery health, charging preferences, memory availability and the ability to pause or disconnect at any time.

Development status: Android cluster computing is a planned SamyStackOS capability, and the Android APK is not yet available for download. Each phone keeps its own memory; the coordinator distributes compatible jobs rather than combining every device into one physical computer. Performance and supported tasks will vary by device.
